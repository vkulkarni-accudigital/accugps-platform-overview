02 · The How

Retrieving & Downloading Video

The camera is always recording — the question is how you get a specific clip out of it. Understand the storage model, then the four ways to pull a clip, then how to download it as evidence.

StorageWhere video lives

Footage can exist in three places at once. Knowing which one you're reaching for decides how you retrieve it.

LocationWhat it holdsNotes
SD CardDeviceThe full continuous recording on the camera, in the vehicle.Largest capacity; varies by card size. Pulled on demand.
CloudServerClips uploaded off the device to AccuGPS servers.Always available from anywhere, even if the vehicle is off.
PCDownloadA downloaded copy saved to your computer.Exported as MP4, encrypted on download.
The two-step modelVideo moves Device (SD) → Cloud → PC. You upload a clip from the SD card to the Cloud (choosing the exact moment and length), then download it from the Cloud to your PC. The Storage location switch — Cloud or Device — tells the Video page which side you're working from.
03The Video workspace — pick the Tracker, set Storage location, choose a window

The four waysPulling a specific clip

1By Time Frame

Search a window

Set a date-and-time range and press Show. Every clip in that window lists out, with an upload-activity timeline marking where footage exists.

2By Time Point

Pull an exact moment

Enter a single timestamp, a length to upload (e.g. 5s) and the channels, then press Upload — the camera sends just that slice to the Cloud.

3From Alerts

Jump from an event

Many alerts carry a video clip. Open the alert and play its footage — a hard-brake or AI event is one click from the clip.

4From Trip Replay

Pull from the route

While replaying a trip, click stop on the route where you want footage — the clip ties to that exact spot on the map.

Method 1 · Time FrameSearch a window of time

  1. With the tracker and storage location set, click Time and pick a From and To on the calendar, then Ok.
  2. Press Show. The activity timeline fills with amber bars where footage exists, and the Cloud videos table lists each clip.
  3. Read each row — start/end, create time, file size, channel, events, status — then act from Operations.
04Amber bars mark where footage exists in the window
05Cloud videos table with the route and per-clip Operations

Method 2 · Time PointPull an exact moment off the SD card

  1. Set the Time field to the precise moment (date + time).
  2. Choose the Length of time to upload — how many seconds to pull (e.g. 5s).
  3. Enter the Channels (road, cabin, or both), then press Upload.
  4. The clip uploads to the Cloud and appears with status Upload Successful.
06Uploading a clip from the device to the Cloud by time point
Extending a clipPulled a clip that cuts off too soon? Use "New" to add the next minute of video onto it — no need to re-find the moment. Handy when an incident runs longer than your first grab.

PhotosSnapshots on the Photo tab

Besides video, the camera captures stills. Switch Camera › Photo to list snapshots for a tracker over a window — same retrieve-and-download flow, lighter files.

07Photo mode — snapshot list with per-image operations
Encrypted downloadsDownloaded MP4s are encrypted — a tamper-evident measure that keeps footage court-defensible. Keep the original download rather than a re-encoded copy when the clip may be used as evidence.
ROI

Four retrieval paths mean the footage you need is never more than a few clicks away — a date, a timestamp, an alert, or a point on the map. A downloadable, encrypted MP4 turns "he said / she said" into a settled claim in minutes.

03 · Safety

AI Camera Alerts

The camera watches the road and the driver with on-device AI and raises an alert the instant it sees risk — tailgating, a lane drift, a distracted or drowsy driver. These land on the AI tab, most carrying an attached clip.

08The Alerts center, AI tab — driver-safety events per tracker

The AI tabFinding what you need

  • Filter by Tracker, Type, and Time (Today, Yesterday, This week, Last week, or custom), then press Show.
  • Each row shows the tracker, time, alert type with icon, full position details with map view, and any note.
  • Manage with Export, Clear All, and Mark All As Read; long feeds paginate.
