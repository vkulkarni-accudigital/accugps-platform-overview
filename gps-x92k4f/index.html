02 · The What

Trip Replay

Play back any tracker's history like a movie. Trip Replay reconstructs a vehicle's route for a chosen window as a breadcrumb trail on the map — with stops, speed violations, alerts, and photos plotted along the way — then lets you animate it and export the data.

Build itRunning a replay

  1. Open Trip Replay from the top navigation, or click Trip replay on any asset card.
  2. Pick the Tracker, then a Time preset (e.g. This week) or a custom From / To.
  3. Optionally set a Daily Starting Time to trim each day to working hours.
  4. Press Show — the route draws on the map and the timeline fills in on the right.
03Setting up a replay — pick the tracker and window, then ShowPlayback

Read itTrail, stops & totals

The route draws as a point-by-point breadcrumb trail with Start and End flags. The timeline on the right alternates driving legs and numbered stops, and the totals summarize the whole window.

  • Breadcrumb trail. The exact path traveled, point by point, from Start flag to End flag.
  • Layer filters. Toggle POI, Stop, Speed violation, Alert, and Photo markers on the route.
  • Stops & events. The timeline alternates driving legs (duration + distance) and numbered stops (dwell time + exact address).
  • Totals. Running Total Distance, Total Stopped Time, and Total Traveled Time.
  • Playback. Animate the vehicle along its route at Slow, Medium, or Fast, with rewind / play / fast-forward to scrub.

03 · Safety

OBD Alerts

OBD alerts are events read directly from the vehicle's OBD / engine bus — harsh driving, ignition changes, tampering, and engine faults. They add engine-level context GPS alone can't see, surface on the Alerts center, and feed the unread badge in the top navigation.

07The Alerts center, OBD tab — engine-level events per tracker on the mapLive feed

The Alerts centerFinding what you need

Open Alerts from the top navigation. Four tabs group the feed — Normal, OBD, DTC, and AI. This walkthrough covers the OBD tab.

  • Filter by Tracker (one device or All), Type (a single alert or All), and Time, then press Show.
  • Each row shows the tracker, time, alert type with icon, and Get Address to resolve coordinates to a street address, plus any note.
  • Manage the feed with Export, Clear All, and Mark All As Read; long feeds paginate.
08Filter by Type — narrow the feed to one OBD alert
09Reading the feed — tracker, time, type & address per row
The 99+ badgeThe number beside Alerts is the unread count across all tabs. A persistent 99+ usually means thresholds are firing more often than expected — tune sensitivity in Settings › Notifications, or filter to the noisiest type to find the cause.
