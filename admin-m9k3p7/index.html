01 · The Fleet menu

Fleet Setup, Scoring & Users

The Fleet menu is about organization: bundle vehicles into fleets that match how you actually operate, measure how each driver is performing, and hand out the logins that let your team in, each scoped to only the vehicles they should see.

1.1

Fleet Setting

Groups

A Fleet is a named group of trackers. Instead of one long vehicle list, you carve the operation into the groups you think in, such as a region, a division, or a service line, and every map view and report can then filter to that fleet.

01Fleet Setting · name a fleet and assign its trackersLive setup
  1. Open Fleet › Setting and give the fleet a clear Name.
  2. In the Tracker list, tick the vehicles that belong to it, or All Trackers for a catch-all.
  3. Press Save. The fleet is now a filter you can reuse across the map and reports.
1.2

Driver Score

Scorecard

Driver Score turns raw driving events into a ranked scorecard. Over a chosen period, it grades behavior so you can coach objectively rather than by anecdote, and see at a glance who is driving safely and who needs a conversation.

02Driver Score · pick a period and score driving behavior
  • Set the window. Choose the Time range and the trackers or drivers to include, then search to build the scorecard.
  • Behavior-based. Scores draw on the same harsh-driving and safety events the OBD and AI alerts raise, so the ranking reflects real road behavior.
  • Coach with evidence. A consistent number per driver makes recognition and correction fair, repeatable, and defensible.
Fed by your alertsThe events that move a Driver Score, harsh acceleration, hard braking, speeding, and the camera's AI flags, are the ones you switch on under Settings › Notifications in Part 2. Tune those first and the score gets sharper.
1.3

User Manager

Logins

User Manager creates the logins that access the platform and decides exactly what each one can see. It has two tabs: Groups for login accounts, and Drivers for the people roster.

03User Manager · a new login scoped to selected trackers
  1. On the Groups tab, enter the Email, Password, and a Group name for the login.
  2. In the Tracker list, tick All Trackers or exactly the vehicles this login should see.
  3. Press Save. The user signs in and sees only their assigned trackers.
GGroups tab

Login accounts

Create and scope the sign-in accounts your dispatchers, managers, and crew leads use.

DDrivers tab

People, not logins

Maintain the driver roster so trips, work hours, and safety events attach to a named person.

ROI

Organized fleets, an objective driver scorecard, and tightly scoped logins mean the platform mirrors how you actually run. Everyone sees the vehicles they own, and driver coaching rests on numbers, not hunches.

02 · The Settings menu

Account, Places, Alerts & Access

The Settings menu is the full configuration surface. Each panel loads into the workspace beside the Live Map. We walk them in the order you would set up a new account: configure the account and devices, map the places that matter, automate alerts and reports, then govern outside access.

2.1

Account & Tracker Management

Config

Start with the settings that shape how everything reads. Under Settings › Account, the basic account settings decide platform-wide display; under Settings › Tracker, you name and dress each device so it is recognizable everywhere.

04Account & Tracker · account preferences and per-device setup
  • Units & formats. US or Metric, 12 or 24-hour clock, and date ordering, feeding every distance, speed, and timestamp.
  • Speed thresholds. Account-wide Slow Speed and Speed Violation values that drive the speed flags in reports.
  • Time Zone & alert recipients. Anchor every timestamp, and add up to five accounts that receive alerts.
  • Per-device identity. Tracker Name, Vehicle, Class, and a map icon, plus Power Saving and Out of Service states.
Set these firstAccount preferences ripple into every report, alert, and map label. Getting units, time zone, and thresholds right on day one saves re-reading every screen later.
2.2

Reporting & Notifications

Rules

Two panels that decide what the numbers mean and what earns your attention. Settings › Reporting sets each vehicle's fuel economy so fuel figures are accurate; Settings › Notifications chooses which events raise an alert and how it reaches you.

05Reporting & Notifications · fuel economy and alert rules
CITY / HWY

Fuel economy

Per-unit city and highway MPG so the Fuel and Summary reports compute gallons correctly.

EVENTS

Alert rules

Toggle each event per tracker, from movement and speeding to the camera's AI safety alarms, each with its own threshold.

DELIVERY

Channel & SOS

Choose Mobile App and/or Email, add up to three SOS contacts, and apply rules fleet-wide.

Tune, don't floodTurning on every alert for every vehicle teaches your team to ignore alerts. Enable the events that require action, set sane thresholds, and let the rest live in the alert log for review.
2.3

Geo-Fencing

Zones

Under Settings › Geo-fence, each row is a named area you care about entries and exits for: a yard, a job site, a customer, a no-go zone. Draw it on the map once and it becomes a reusable, alerting place.

06Geo-fence · draw zones and set entry / exit alerts
  • Current Vehicles & Month visits. How many vehicles are inside right now, and the visit count this month.
  • Notifications. Per-zone alert mode: Off, entry, exit, or Both.
  • Trackers & Operations. Scope each zone to the relevant vehicles; edit, share, or delete per row.
Feeds the Geo-fence visit reportEvery zone you define here becomes selectable in the Geo-fence visit report (Reports walkthrough), which lists who entered and their dwell time. Set the fence once, report on it forever.
2.4

Point of Interest

Pins

Under Settings › Point Of Interest, a POI is a single pinned location with a proximity range. Use it to mark depots, fuel stops, or customer sites and know when a vehicle is near one.

07Point of Interest · pinned landmarks with a proximity range
  • Name & Address. A label and the geocoded address for the pin.
  • Icon & Range (ft). A colored map icon and a radius that defines "near."
  • Notifications & Trackers. Optional proximity alerts (Off or Both), scoped to chosen trackers.
Geo-fence vs. POIUse a Geo-fence for an area and entry/exit events. Use a Point of Interest for proximity to a point within a set radius. They complement each other.
2.5

Report Scheduling

Schedule

Under Settings › Report Tasks, schedule any report to generate and email automatically, so no one has to remember to pull it. Each task is one row you can pause, edit, or delete.

08Report Tasks · report, trackers, frequency & recipients
  1. Press Add, then pick the Report to send, such as Summary or Maintenance.
  2. Select the Trackers it covers, and set the Time, for example Every day.
  3. Add up to five recipient Emails, then Save. The task appears in the list, running on its own.
2.6

Shared Accounts

Access

Under Settings › Shared Account, grant another AccuGPS account access to specific trackers of yours, ideal for an installer, a partner, or a customer who has their own login. Access is always scoped to the vehicles you choose.

09Shared Account · external accounts and the trackers shared to each
  • Account tab. Lists each external email and how many trackers are shared with it; Add Account to invite a new one.
  • Add Tracker. Per-row control to share specific vehicles, and set the function and time window they get.
  • Tracker tab. The mirror view: per tracker, which accounts it is shared with.
User Manager vs. Shared AccountChoose by who needs access. Your own team uses User Manager logins inside your account. A separate company or person with their own AccuGPS account uses Shared Account. Both stay scoped to specific trackers.
ROI

Configured once, this layer pays out on every screen after: accurate fuel numbers, alerts that fire only when they should, reports that arrive on their own, and outside access that never exceeds what you granted. The platform runs itself, on your rules.
